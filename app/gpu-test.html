WebGPU compute self-test

Validates js/gpu/fft3d.js, cosmo3d.js, and the GPU-resident nLPT pipeline (lpt3d.js: term build, Dsc, Legendre inversion with the transverse correction; sheet3d.js: tetrahedral sheet, its P1 (vertex-interpolated) variant and the exact clipped deposits; the dual-sheet density of the Hopf–Cole inverse map in cosmo3d.js) against the WASM core and times each stage. URL options (comma lists of grid sizes): ?auto=1&val=32,64&bench=64,128&lval=32,64&lbench=64,128&wo128=4; val/bench: FFT, positions, CIC, Hopf–Cole, spectra; lval: nLPT checks (EdS 4LPT and ΛCDM Ωm = 0.3 3LPT); lbench: nLPT stage timings; wo128: LPT order of the WASM reference at 128³ (0 = skip); skipold=1 skips the older checks. Exact (clipping-based) sheet deposits (exact2d.js: triangles, exact3d.js + sheet3d.js: tetrahedra, P0 and P1): per-cell moments of the 70 r3d fixtures (fixtures_r3d.txt, Julia R3D.jl reference), full deposits against WASM sheet_density_exact (exval3=32,64, exval2=128,256) and timings (exbench2=256,512, exbench3=64,128); onlyexact=1 runs only these, noexact=1 skips them, nofix=1 skips the fixtures.
Direct (deposit-free) sheet spectra (direct.js: divided differences of the exponential over the simplices, P0 and P1, two-stage reduction): per-mode checks against the f64 JS port of core/src/direct.rs (dval2=32, dval3=16), against Gauss–Legendre quadrature of the simplex integrals (dquad2=16, dquad3=8), binned spectra against WASM direct_spectrum with the same lattice modes (dwasm=64; P1 takes ~10 s in WASM) and timings with 20 bins × 128 modes (dbench2=256,512, dbench3=64,128); onlydirect=1 runs only these, nodirect=1 skips them.
